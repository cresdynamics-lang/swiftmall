SWIFT MALL - HERO SOURCE IMAGES
================================
Clean, full-resolution replacements for the low-quality banner screenshots.
All photos are from Pexels (pexels.com) and are free to use on a commercial website under the Pexels licence. Attribution is not required but is welcome. Each file name ends with its Pexels photo ID so you can find the original page: pexels.com/photo/<ID>.

Folders (8 categories, 66 photos, about 2400 px wide, JPG):
01_Shoppers_with_bags    lifestyle shoppers with shopping bags (the lady carrying products)
02_Headphones            product shots
03_Sneakers              product shots
04_Handbags              product shots
05_Smartwatches          product shots (some with blank green screens, good for mockups)
06_TV_and_living_room    TV and smart-home scenes
07_Speakers              portable speakers
08_Gaming_and_VR         controllers and VR headset

How they are meant to be used
- The photos are used as clean images inside the Swift Mall carousel. Backgrounds, text, prices and badges are added by the website in the Swift Mall colours (black, yellow, white), not baked into the photos.
- Names ending in _portrait, _square or _wide tell you the shape, so you can pick the right crop for desktop or phone.

Please read before publishing
1. People: do not use a photo of a person in a way that suggests they endorse Swift Mall or a product.
2. Brand logos: some photos show visible brand marks (for example Marshall, JBL, Bose, Nike, Converse, MIVI). Use those only if Swift Mall actually sells that brand, or crop them out.
3. Swap in real stock: once Swift Mall has its own product photos, replace these. Real photos of real stock on sale convert better and avoid any mismatch with what the customer receives.
4. Not included: smartphones and kitchen appliances (juicer). Phones can use the Apple and Samsung product images we already have; a juicer needs a source that allows it, or Swift Mall's own photo.
